## MT. The twin method: how every NEW scene is made (since 29 Oct 2026)
This section wins over the older routes below whenever they disagree.
- **The canon is frozen.** Max = BO-6, TA-1…TA-6, the approved emotions and the 8 approved twin frames in `assets/twins/`. A new frame never becomes a reference, however good: every take is judged against this set, so Max cannot drift.
- **Every new scene has a twin**: the approved frame closest in pose, expression and composition (`references/prompts.md` §6). Write the prompt from §6 and attach: the twin's Max crop → the whole twin frame → BO-6 → the cast frame → the medal crop. **The twin choice moves the pass rate more than any wording:** for Max sitting beside a standing adult use G4, never G1.
- **Camera at the eye height of the people in the scene** (standing adults: standing eye level, looking slightly down at Max; seated adults: low). Write the SIZE line, whom Max looks at, and "in the same bright sunlight as the people, NOT in shade".
- **4 takes, one pass each, and no edits on Max afterwards.** Never resize, cut out, paste, re-light or re-add Max, and never edit his face or mouth on a new frame: the brand owner rejected every edited frame on 29 Oct ("pasted in", bulging eyes after a mouth edit). A wrong take is discarded, not fixed.
- **Drop the clear rejects yourself** before showing anything (`references/pipeline.md` §A0 step 6), then build the review board with `scripts/twin_board.py` (twin crop next to each take's Max). The owner picks one take per scene, or none; a scene with no good take is replaced or simplified, never patched.
- **Do not judge "is this our Max" yourself, and do not let an agent do it.** On 29 Oct a rubric-scoring agent agreed with the owner on 11 of 20 frames and an image-similarity score did not separate his yes from his no at all. You filter clear rejects; the owner decides.
- **Tested and rejected (29 Oct), do not propose them again as the fix:** the resize script and any paste of Max; a model re-light of a pasted Max; a layout-guided re-add; AI 3D models of Max (Meshy, Tripo: a pale figurine, two medals, wrong face); a trained Higgsfield Soul (keeps the face but not the size, sometimes two dogs, and cannot include the approved cast).
- **Known weak spot:** next to kneeling or crouching adults, people sitting on the ground, and when lying, Max still comes out too big or of uncertain size. Adults on a bench, chair, wall or tailgate work with the chest anchor (`references/prompts.md` §6); standing adults work best.
- **Results so far:** R7 (8 new scenes × 4 takes): the owner found a usable take in 6 of 8 scenes; 17 of 32 takes canonical. The older edit-and-fix routes reached 2 of 20 accepted frames. **Max sitting beside a standing adult, twin G4 and the §6 prompt (30 Oct):** 13 of 19 good takes on the scenes the prompt was tuned on, 12 of 19 on five new scenes, a usable take in every scene. These numbers cover that one card only; every other card is measured separately before its numbers are quoted. **Max beside seated adults (twins G3 / F1, chest anchor):** 10 of 19 good, every scene with a usable take (R14, 30 Oct). **Max trotting on a leash (twin L02, size named by the twin's proportion):** 15 of 18 good, every scene with a usable take (R16, 30 Oct). **Max beside a toddler (twin L06):** 9 of 19 good, every scene with a usable take, Max sometimes a little big (R17, 30 Oct).

## Prompt skeleton (copy exactly; details in `references/prompts.md`)
Every new Max frame is written in this order and in English. The texts below are copied from `references/prompts.md`; paste them **character for character**. Never paraphrase the Max block, never drop the Element tag, never leave a `{slot}` or a bracketed placeholder in the final prompt. If a part does not fit the scene, read `references/prompts.md` before changing anything.

1. **Camera:** `Candid documentary photograph, handheld at eye level, 35mm lens, f/2.8.` On a location plate use `Camera exactly as in the first reference photo.` instead.
2. **Style line (Daylight; Cinema and Evening are relights, prompt 5.6):** `CLEAN DAYLIGHT: bright natural late-morning daylight from one clear direction with real shadows, neutral daylight white balance, true whites, clean blue sky, fresh greens, natural colours, crisp digital capture, no grain.`
3. **Scene:** 2–4 sentences: the moment, one action with a consequence, two story objects, nobody looks at the lens. On a plate (`references/plates.md`), begin with `In the exact location of the first reference photo (keep the place, camera and light exactly)…`.
4. **Max block** (fill POSE, EXPRESSION, MOUTH and SCALE; keep everything else word for word):

```text
{POSE: where he is and how he is placed, **beside {named person} and half a step BEHIND them, slightly farther from the camera than {named person}, never closer** (NOT in the foreground)}: <<<01ba1fc5-227c-439d-aa29-47ada4e45545>>> — Max, the SMA mascot, looking EXACTLY like the attached master photo (same face, same fur length and texture, same colours, same proportions); use the master photo for his LOOK ONLY, NOT for his pose, head angle or camera angle: a stylised Pixar-quality 3D animated golden retriever composited into this real photograph, clearly an animated character, NOT a real dog. Fully grown ADULT male of about 30 kg, NOT a puppy, with the body proportions of the attached SIDE-VIEW photo of Max (his whole body in profile): long legs (front leg from elbow to ground about half his shoulder height), deep chest, body clearly longer than it is tall, long tapered muzzle (almost half the length of his head), a SMALL head: sitting, his head from crown to chin is only about one fifth of his sitting height; NOT a big round puppy head, NOT short stubby legs, NOT chubby. Face as in his front close-up reference: refined, fairly narrow muzzle. Cartoon brown eyes with visible whites (medium-small, not puppy eyes), both pupils centred and looking the same way, oversized matte dark-brown nose, soft drop ears. Navy blue leather collar with ONE ROUND brushed-brass MEDAL tag hanging from a small ring at the front: a flat coin-shaped disc with a raised rim and a small embossed letter M in its centre, about as wide as his nose; NOT a letter-shaped M charm, NOT a cut-out letter, NOT square, NOT shiny gold. Fur: dense, soft, MATTE feature-animation groom with fine strands and natural clumping, honey-gold back fading to cream chest; NOT glossy, NOT plastic, NOT a plush toy. Expression: {EXPRESSION, §4}. {MOUTH: for an open-mouth emotion write `Mouth softly open, pink tongue just showing.`; for a closed-mouth emotion write `His mouth stays CLOSED: lips together, no tongue.`} SCALE: {SCALE line, §3} His size relative to the people is exactly as in the attached cast reference photo: sitting, the top of his head is at a standing adult's hip, a little over half of that adult's height; his paws stand on the same ground line as that adult's shoes, NOT lower in the frame, NOT nearer the camera. Soft contact shadow, lit by the same light as the scene. He is the only stylised element and the only warm-coloured object in the frame.
```

   - **EXPRESSION** (one of the 8, word for word): **EX-01 joy**: `pure joy: mouth open in a big happy cartoon grin, pink tongue out and slightly to one side, a short row of small rounded even white teeth along the lower jaw (no pointed fangs), eyes OPEN and bright with clearly visible whites (NOT squinting), brows lifted happily, ears relaxed back` · **EX-02 head tilt**: `curious head tilt: head tilted about 25 degrees to one side, one ear lifted slightly higher than the other, a small calm closed-mouth smile, eyes bright and interested (not startled, not worried); a grown-up dog's curiosity, NOT puppyish` · **EX-03 chin on paws**: `lying flat on his belly with his chin resting on his crossed front paws, eyes open and looking calmly, CONTENT: relaxed brows, a small closed-mouth smile, ears soft; calm and happy, NOT sad, NOT pleading` · **EX-04 excited**: `excited: mouth open in a big grin with the tongue showing, a short row of small rounded even white teeth along the lower jaw (no pointed fangs), ears perked forward, tail held high and wagging (slight motion blur on the tail tip)` · **EX-06 trot**: `happy trot on all four long legs: mouth slightly open, ears bouncing, one front paw and the opposite hind paw lifted, tail streaming behind, a little motion blur on his paws` · **EX-07 dozing**: `peacefully dozing and CONTENT: eyes gently closed in two happy curves, a small relaxed smile, head resting on his front paws; a cosy happy nap, NOT sad, NOT sick` · **EX-08 listening**: `listening attentively: ears raised and pushed forward, head turned slightly toward the person, eyes focused on them, mouth closed` · **EX-10 suspicious**: `playfully suspicious: ONE eyebrow raised high, the other lowered, eyes in a sideways glance at {object}, a small knowing smirk with the mouth closed; comic and friendly, NOT angry`
   - **MOUTH**: open-mouth emotions (EX-01, EX-04, EX-06) `Mouth softly open, pink tongue just showing.`; all others `His mouth stays CLOSED: lips together, no tongue.` Teeth in these emotions are fine.
   - **SCALE** (pick the one that fits, anchor it to an object in the frame): **sit**: `real golden-retriever size; sitting, the top of his head is at a standing adult's hip, about 80 cm from the ground; next to a seated adult his head is no higher than their lower chest.` · **sit-kitchen**: `a FULL-SIZE ADULT golden retriever of about 30 kg, NOT a puppy: sitting, the top of his head is level with the kitchen counter / tabletop (about 78 cm) and with the standing adult's hip.` · **stand**: `standing, his shoulders are just above a standing adult's knee (about 58 cm) and his head reaches mid-thigh.` · **lie**: `lying, nose to tail-base he is about 1 metre long, roughly an adult's leg from hip to heel. Stretched out, NOT curled into a ball, long front legs forward.` · **car**: `standing beside a car wheel, his back is just below the top of the tyre.` More keys in `references/prompts.md` §3.
   - **POSE**: where he is and how he is placed, always `beside {the named person} and half a step BEHIND them, slightly farther from the camera than {the named person}, never closer (NOT in the foreground)`; never between the people and the camera. Do not ask for a minimum share of the frame: in the 26 Oct control set "filling at least a third of the frame height" made him too big in 7 of 10 frames. A small head in a wide shot gets a face pass (M4).
   - Right after the Max block (before the cast line), add the fur line for Daylight: `his fur is a warm honey-gold (yellow-gold, cream chest) like the master photo, NOT orange, NOT red-brown`.

## M3. Size and body
- Size = an **object anchor** in the frame ("top of his head level with the counter / high-chair tray", "top of his head at the dad's hip") **and** "beside {person} and half a step behind them, never closer to the camera" (NOT in the foreground). The cast reference photo (SC-1 / SC-3) shows the approved size: sitting, his crown at the standing mom's hip, 0.55–0.57 of her height, paws on her shoe line.
- Against the puppy look, describe the **body** as ratios: head about 1/5 of sitting height, muzzle almost half the head, long legs, stretched out not curled. "Adult" and "30 kg" alone gave a puppy (22 Oct, F4).
- Size check (`references/qa-checklist.md` row 4, measured with `scripts/ruler.py`): paws on the named adult's shoe line (never lower in the frame = never nearer the camera); sitting, crown to paws 0.50–0.60 of that standing adult's height; standing 0.42–0.52; lying, skull without ears ≈ the adult's head width, nose to tail-base ≈ 0.9–1 m. Do NOT judge by head height: his head with ears is about as tall as an adult's (SC-1). The usual failure is not a bigger dog but a dog placed in front of the people, which reads bigger (control set, 26 Oct: 6 of 10 frames).
- **Size or place wrong → discard the take and generate again with the twin method (MT).** The resize-without-a-model route (`scripts/resize_max.py`, pipeline step 8a) is withdrawn: on 29 Oct the brand owner rejected every frame it produced as pasted in. Its "20 of 20" size result measured the ratio, not whether the frame looked real.
- An edit never changes scale or pose. For those: remove Max, then re-add on the clean plate (`references/pipeline.md` step 8), with "same framing, no zoom, no crop".
- The tag is ONE ROUND brass MEDAL with a small embossed M (`references/max-model.md` §2); write it out in full. "M tag" alone gave a letter-shaped M charm (22 Oct).

## M4. Face and emotion
- On a NEW frame do not edit the face: discard the take (MT). The fixes below are kept for already approved frames and their relights only.
- Expressions on new frames: only what the approved twins show (calm closed smile looking at a person, happy open mouth with the tongue just showing, asleep). The owner rejected EX-08 listening (reads sad) and raised brows with wide eyes (29 Oct).
- Only the 8 emotions. Eye whites visible except in sleep. **Teeth are allowed** when they come with an approved emotion (joy, excited, trot): do not spend an edit pass on them. Calm states in positive words ("CONTENT, relaxed brows, small smile, NOT sad"). No strong emotion words that fold the face.
- A wrong face is fixed **with a picture**: a crop around Max (whole head + collar), an approved head from `assets/refs/` with the **same expression** and a similar angle as the 2nd image, "REPLACE the dog's head … KEEP THE HEAD EXACTLY AS LARGE AS NOW", then `FULL=1 python3 scripts/paste_crop.py …`. Never blend two faces pixel by pixel.
- **Wide shots: plan a face pass.** In a wide family frame Max's head is small (under ~400 px) and often in profile, and the model then draws a realistic dog face: small eye without a white, long plain muzzle, small nose. The brand owner rejected all three treatments of S1 for this (22 Oct). Check the face at 100% against TA-5 / TA-6 before anything else, and fix it on a crop with an approved head at the SAME angle: profile → `MX3-TA-6-head-profile.jpg` (or P02A, P08D), three-quarter → P06A, front → TA-5. A reference with a side glance (P03A, P06C) on a small head gave a smooth "clay" head with squinting side-eyes: do not use those for a profile head. If the edit also repaints the background inside the crop, paste back only the head: `REGION=x0,y0,x1,y1 python3 scripts/paste_crop.py …` (box in crop pixels). Make the relights only from the fixed Daylight frame. Worked example, approved by the brand owner on 22 Oct: board `assets/boards/15-wide-shot-face-pass.jpg`.
- Eyes: both pupils centred, both eyes looking the same way. A side glance in the reference copies over and reads cross-eyed; fix it with the eye reference `assets/refs/HEAD-TA3-eyes-straight.jpg`, not with words.
- When the head is small (< ~500 px), do every face or mouth edit on a crop around Max (whole head + collar, with margin), never on the full frame; then paste back with `scripts/paste_crop.py`.

## M4b. What the prompt alone does not hold (kit test, 20 Oct: 12 frames)
- The right Max came out 12 of 12; a frame with no notes on Max only 6 of 12. With the twin method make 4 takes and pick; do not plan a fix pass on a new frame (MT).
- Fill the MOUTH slot of the Max block to match the expression (`references/prompts.md` §2). A fixed open-mouth line overrode closed-mouth expressions in 4 of 8 frames (22 Oct).
- Max placed on the camera side of people or furniture comes out too big: place him beside or behind a named person.
- Poses like "chin on paws" or "not curled" are often ignored: describe the head position physically, or attach a "pose only" image. EX-03 chin on paws failed 4 of 4 in the English test (22 Oct): when head-down matters, use EX-07 or EX-08 instead.
- Fix passes: at most 2 per frame (each pass smooths fur). If it still fails, remove + re-add or regenerate.
- Relights: 2 takes each; see the relight notes in `references/prompts.md` §5.6 (name the ground outdoors, mount lamps on real objects).

## M5. Integration
- Max is lit by the scene's light, has a contact shadow, is no brighter or more saturated than the real objects, wears a NAVY collar with a dull-brass round medal tag. He stays stylised; never make him realistic to "fit".
- Name every glass, fog or water layer as "keep" in an edit.
- A 4k full-frame edit can come back with a colour cast: run `scripts/colormatch.py` against the approved frame (undo, not a grade). No grain, no grade otherwise.

## M5b. Colour by light and the three treatments
- Every scene ships in **Daylight, Cinema and Evening**. When someone asks for a Cinema or Evening frame of a NEW scene, give both steps: first the Daylight generation prompt (skeleton above), then the relight prompt 5.6 to run on the approved Daylight frame. Approve Daylight first; make Cinema and Evening by **relighting that same frame** (prompt 5.6). A new generation with the Daylight frame as reference drifts redder: do not use it.
- Max's fur (median hue of the mid-tone fur, `references/prompts.md` §3c, board `assets/boards/08-max-colour-by-light.jpg`): Daylight **28–33°** honey-gold (BO-6 ≈ 30–31°) · Cinema **26.5–30.5°** golden-amber · Evening **19–28°** on the lit side: honey-gold with warm amber highlights where the lamp falls, darker in shadow, never orange all over (board 08's swatch label "orange-amber" means those highlights). **Below 19° reads red-brown: reject.**
- Judge colour by eye next to BO-6, not by the number alone: too saturated reads orange even inside the band.
- Evening relight: say "the same light over the whole frame, no sunlight anywhere" and where each lamp is; never write "his fur glows orange-amber" (it makes him orange). Write "gentle warm highlights; his fur stays honey-gold, NOT orange, NOT red-brown".
- Off-colour Max: `scripts/recolor_max.py --mask` with a whole-silhouette mask from `scripts/max_mask.py` (hue/saturation of his fur only, no model). A partial mask leaves red patches. **Never fix colour with a model edit**: on 21 Oct it painted cream bibs, white muzzles and a realistic head.
- Evening: light the people from real lamps above them (a street lamp over the sidewalk, ceiling lights over a kitchen) and ask for both faces lit equally; otherwise one face goes dark or the frame goes too dark.
- Every relight prompt says Max stays EXACTLY the same cartoon character (eyes with whites, face, fur pattern: no lighter chest/belly/muzzle, not a real dog).
- Before choosing any take: Max's head and fur pattern next to the approved frame at 100%. Never choose from thumbnails.
